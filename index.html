Iyawo Institute 52-Week Sacred Formation

The original 16-week Modified Khemetian–Ifá Tree of Life course now becomes a full 52-week priesthood formation cycle. The year is organized through four seasonal gates: Ilekes at the Spring Equinox, Warriors at the Summer Solstice, Hand of Ifá at the Autumn Equinox, and Priesthood Crowning at the Winter Solstice.

From 16 Weeks to 52 Weeks

The 16-week roadmap remains the root image and public reference. The 52-week structure gives the initiate more time to practice, reflect, demonstrate, and become accountable before receiving greater spiritual responsibility.

Radical Devotion

Auset / Yemojah

Devotion, alignment, care, restoration, humility, and sacred responsibility. This current becomes visible through conduct, consistency, and relationship to community.

Words of Power

Sekert / Olokun

Speech, mystery, depth, protection, silence, invocation, and transformation. This current teaches that words must clarify, heal, protect, and align.

Infinite Wisdom

Tehuti / Orunmila

Discernment, divination, destiny, order, memory, and spiritual intelligence. This current requires the student to consult wisdom before action.

Solstice & Equinox Index

Each major initiation gate is indexed to a seasonal doorway. The initiate’s year is therefore not only academic; it is cosmological, ritual, and seasonal.

Spring Equinox

Ilekes Gate

Identity, devotion, spiritual orientation, sacred belonging, and visible covenant.

  • Weeks 1–13
  • Root current: Radical Devotion
  • Theme: I belong, therefore I behave with character.
Summer Solstice

Warriors Gate

Protection, boundaries, courage, vows, accountability, and disciplined action.

  • Weeks 14–26
  • Root current: Words of Power
  • Theme: I am protected because I am disciplined.
Autumn Equinox

Hand of Ifá Gate

Wisdom, destiny alignment, divination readiness, sacrifice, and sacred listening.

  • Weeks 27–39
  • Root current: Infinite Wisdom
  • Theme: I consult wisdom before I act.
Winter Solstice

Priesthood Crowning

Integration, elder review, ritual responsibility, public service, and crowning preparation.

  • Weeks 40–52
  • Root current: Royal Legacy
  • Theme: I carry light through the longest night.

52 Week Course Map

The 52 weeks may be taught through weekly lessons, Google Classroom posts, same-repo pages, or protected modules. Each quarter contains study, practice, reflection, divination, and review.

Weeks 1–4 Orientation, SGI vows, white-cloth consciousness, Ori alignment, ancestral grounding, and daily practice.
Weeks 5–8 Radical Devotion, Auset/Yemojah, character formation, humility, sacred relationship, and visible discipline.
Weeks 9–13 Ilekes preparation, elder review, devotional accountability, and Spring Equinox gate completion.
Weeks 14–17 Warrior orientation, protection, boundaries, spiritual courage, and the correct use of power.
Weeks 18–21 Ogun, Ochosi, Eshu, Osun, vows, sacrifice, speech, correction, and disciplined action.
Weeks 22–26 Ajagun versus Ajogun, accountability, Warrior readiness, and Summer Solstice gate completion.
Weeks 27–30 Hand of Ifá orientation, Tehuti/Orunmila, Odu seniority, humility before wisdom, and listening practice.
Weeks 31–35 Divination ethics, Obi and Four Cowrie foundations, Odu orientation, sacrifice logic, and journaling.
Weeks 36–39 Wisdom demonstration, destiny alignment, Hand of Ifá review, and Autumn Equinox gate completion.
Weeks 40–43 Priesthood responsibility, public conduct, ritual speech, sacred boundaries, and service posture.
Weeks 44–48 Demonstration cycle: readings, offerings, reflection, correction, teaching posture, and community service.
Weeks 49–52 Final integration, elder assessment, divination confirmation, Winter Solstice crowning gate, and next-step assignment.
