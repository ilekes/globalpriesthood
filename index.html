52 Week Sacred Formation Course

Iyawo University expands from a 16-week overview into a full 52-week process of study, devotion, divination, character formation, and demonstrated priestly readiness. The four major gates — Ilekes, Warriors, Hand of Ifa, and Priesthood Crowning — are indexed to the solstice and equinox cycle.

Enter the 52 Week Structure View Solstice / Equinox Index

A year of white-cloth consciousness, disciplined practice, divination ethics, ancestral alignment, and sacred accountability.

The 52 Week Design

The year is organized into four 13-week quarters. Each quarter builds a different spiritual capacity and concludes near a seasonal gate. The structure allows the Iyawo to mature gradually while remaining accountable to lesson study, ritual conduct, divination practice, and elder review.

Weeks 1–13

Spring Gate: Ilekes

Foundation, devotion, identity, daily discipline, Ori alignment, and the first visible covenant of belonging.

Weeks 14–26

Summer Gate: Warriors

Protection, boundaries, sacred courage, vow-keeping, accountability, and the proper distinction between Ajagun and Ajogun.

Weeks 27–39

Autumn Gate: Hand of Ifa

Wisdom, divination discipline, Odu orientation, ethical speech, sacrifice, and service to destiny.

Weeks 40–52

Winter Gate: Priesthood Crowning

Integration, demonstration, elder review, ritual readiness, community service, and crowning preparation.

Solstice & Equinox Index

The rites are not treated as isolated events. Each is indexed to a seasonal doorway so that the student’s spiritual development follows the rhythm of the year.

Spring Equinox

Ilekes Gate

  • Orientation into sacred identity
  • Receiving the visible sign of devotion
  • Beginning the daily discipline of white-cloth consciousness
  • Core theme: “I belong, therefore I behave with character.”
Summer Solstice

Warriors Gate

  • Strengthening spiritual boundaries
  • Understanding protection, direction, and accountability
  • Working with Ogun, Ochosi, Eshu, and Osun
  • Core theme: “I am protected because I am disciplined.”
Autumn Equinox

Hand of Ifa Gate

  • Entering wisdom study and divination discipline
  • Learning to listen before speaking
  • Aligning sacrifice, Odu, destiny, and service
  • Core theme: “I consult wisdom before I act.”
Winter Solstice

Priesthood Crowning Gate

  • Demonstrating readiness before elders
  • Integrating devotion, power, wisdom, and service
  • Preparing for public responsibility
  • Core theme: “I carry light through the longest night.”

52 Week Course Map

Who May Enter

Iyawo University is designed for initiates and serious students under elder guidance. Participation assumes reverence, humility, consistency, and a willingness to be corrected.

How Progress Is Confirmed

Advancement is based on lesson completion, daily practice, divination confirmation, elder review, and demonstrated character — not speed alone.

What the Year Produces

The goal is not simply information. The goal is formation: devotion, protection, wisdom, service, and readiness to carry sacred responsibility.
